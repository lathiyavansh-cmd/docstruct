# Canteen Feedback Kiosk

## Abstract

Students complain about canteen food, but the canteen committee hears only a few of those complaints. This project places a tablet kiosk at the canteen exit where students rate their meal in five seconds.

## Introduction

The canteen committee meets once a month and relies on a suggestion box that is rarely used. Feedback arrives late and cannot be linked to a specific dish or day.

## Problem Statement

Collect quick, honest feedback on each meal and show the committee which dishes and days need attention.

## Literature Review

Restaurant chains use exit kiosks with three or five faces to rate a visit. Studies show response rates above 30 percent when rating takes under ten seconds, compared with under 2 percent for paper forms.

## Proposed System

The kiosk shows today's menu. A student taps a dish and a rating from one to five. The committee sees a weekly dashboard of ratings per dish and day.

## Implementation

The kiosk is a web page running full-screen on a tablet. Ratings are sent to a small server and stored in a database; no login is needed and no personal data is kept.

## Results

In two weeks the kiosk collected 1,840 ratings. Two dishes rated below 2.5 were changed, and their replacements averaged 3.9.

## Conclusion

A five-second rating at the exit gave the committee more feedback in two weeks than the suggestion box gave in a year.

# Library Seat Booking: Project Proposal

## 1 Introduction

Students currently walk to the library to find out whether a reading-room seat is free. This proposal describes a small web application that lets students see free seats and book one for a fixed slot before they arrive.

## 2 Objectives

The system should let a student book a seat up to 24 hours in advance. A booking lasts 2 hours and may be extended once if the seat is still free. Students who do not arrive within 10 minutes of the slot start lose the booking.

## 3 Scope

The first release covers the two reading rooms on the ground floor (120 seats). Group study rooms are out of scope.

## 4 Functional Requirements

4.1 The system should show a live map of seats with their status: free, booked or occupied.

4.2 A student should be able to cancel a booking at any time before the slot starts.

4.3 The librarian should be able to block a seat for maintenance.

## 5 Timeline

The prototype will be ready by 15 December 2026, and the pilot in the North reading room will run for four weeks from January 2027.
